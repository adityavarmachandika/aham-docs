# Notebook Notes

The current canvas was created from 11 handwritten pages.

## How visual marks were interpreted

- Stars and asterisks were grouped into later ideas.
- Boxes used as normal flow-diagram nodes remain diagram nodes.
- Emphasized boxes that look future-oriented were placed under later ideas.
- Crossed-out text was not treated as rejected unless its meaning was clear.
- Unclear words were not silently completed.
