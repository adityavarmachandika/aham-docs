## How visual marks were interpreted

- Stars and asterisks usually indicate emphasis or future direction.
- Boxes used as normal flow-diagram nodes remain diagram nodes.
- Emphasized boxes that look future-oriented are kept as later or conditional ideas.
- Crossed-out text is not treated as rejected unless its meaning is clear.
- Unclear words are not silently completed.
